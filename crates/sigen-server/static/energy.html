How to read these costs

Rates include VAT. Times and billing days use Europe/London. Completed-day costs come from Home Assistant and include its reported standing charge.

Live grid costs show the cost or revenue per hour if the current power and rate continued. They exclude standing charges. They are not today’s bill.

Solar and load comparisons use today’s flat import rate where the entire day is covered. Solar value is the cost of buying the same energy; it is not measured savings. Load value assumes all load was bought from the grid and excludes standing charges. Battery energy cannot be priced reliably without knowing its charging source and time.

Today’s grid bill is unavailable: the inverter exposes cumulative grid counters rather than daily import/export totals. Variable tariffs require energy usage for each rate interval; daily totals alone cannot determine their cost. Missing export rates are never treated as zero.
